2. CAI TIEN & TOI UU CHUAN 144 FPS:
-----------------------------------
 - FRAME TIME BUDGET (6.94 ms):
   Chu ky 144 FPS = 1000 / 144 = 6.9444 ms. Deadline ghep manh la 5.0 ms
   (72% chu ky), du 1.94 ms cho giai ma (~0.5 ms TurboJPEG) va callback.
   Khong khuyen nghi dat 144 FPS cao hon voi receiver deadline 5.0 ms: o
   240 FPS chu ky chi con 4.17 ms, ngan hon deadline, nen mot manh rot co
   the song qua chu ky ke tiep va lam tang nguy co ghep nham frame.

 - DEADLINE TIMEOUT PHAN MANH UDP (5.0 ms):
   Bo ghep manh (Frame Reassembler) huy frame do dut cap/rot goi sau 5.0 ms
   ke tu manh dau tien, don sach truoc khi frame tiep theo toi. Zero lag
   tich luy, khong co hien tuong frame cu chiem bo nho.

 - DONG BO CHUAN HSV MAU TIM CHO COLOR TARGET:
   + H: [120, 160]
   + S >= 188
   + V >= 115
   (Day la nguong bot dung de nhan dien; plugin chi bien doi mau.)

 - TI LE CHUAN 1:1:
   320x320 giup Color Target khong bi mat dau khi dich ngoai, nhay hoac
   khi giat sung.

 - RECEIVER TACH LUONG & GIAI MA TURBOJPEG SIMD:
   + Luong mang doc socket buffer 8 MB, uu tien cao nhat.
   + Hang doi size-1 (LatestFrameSlot) giup frame render luon la frame moi
     nhat; frame chua kip giai ma bi ghi de, khong bao gio ton dong.
   + Giai ma TurboJPEG ~0.5 ms (OpenCV fallback neu thieu DLL).
   + Bat Windows Timer 1 ms (timeBeginPeriod).

4. FIREWALL - TAO TREN MAY NAO:
-------------------------------
  MAY 1 (OBS) - bat buoc:
    New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "CVM xUDP - Beep 4455 In" `
      -Direction Inbound -Protocol UDP -LocalPort 4455 -Action Allow
    New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "CVM xUDP - Stream 5000 Out" `
      -Direction Outbound -Protocol UDP -RemotePort 5000 -Action Allow

  MAY 2 (bot) - bat buoc:
    New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "CVM xUDP - Stream 5000 In" `
      -Direction Inbound -Protocol UDP -LocalPort 5000 -Action Allow

  Luu y: rule outbound phai dung -RemotePort 5000 (OBS gui tu cong nguon
  ngau nhien toi PC2:5000). Dung -LocalPort 5000 cho outbound la sai.

  CANH BAO: KHONG chay receiver_ultra_fast.py DONG THOI voi bot tren cung

  Receiver v2.1 chi chap nhan 2 dang goi:
    (a) single-packet: ca JPEG trong 1 datagram, bat dau bang FF D8
    (b) fragment 8 byte header [total_size u32][frag_idx u16][total_frags u16]
  Luong byte tho khong header KHONG duoc ho tro nua.

8. CHAN DOAN NHANH:
-------------------
  1. FPS = 0, Delay = 0:
     - OBS Target Port co dung 5000?
     - Bot udp_port co dung 5000 (Capture -> UDP)?
     - Tren PC2:  netstat -ano | findstr :5000   -> co process bot khong?
     - Firewall PC2 da mo UDP 5000 In chua?
     - Hai may cung subnet? Ping duoc?
     - Log bot co dong "[UDP] listening on 0.0.0.0:5000"?
       Neu co "[UDP] bind ... failed" -> dang co process khac chiem cong.
  2. Co frame nhung meo / xanh / dut:
     - Xem stats: decode_errors, bad_frames, oversize_packets, timeout_drops.
     - TurboJPEG DLL dung 64-bit? (C:\libjpeg-turbo64\bin\turbojpeg.dll)
     - JPEG quality qua cao -> frame > 65 KB -> kiem tra fragment.
  3. Beep khong keu:
     - audio_feedback_enabled = true?
     - audio_feedback_ip = IP LAN cua PC1?
     - Firewall PC1 da mo UDP 4455 In chua?
     - toggle_feedback_method = "udp" (hoac "zmq" -> se fallback va log).
  4. Bind fail:
     - Dong receiver_ultra_fast.py / bot instance khac.
     - Doi port o CA OBS va bot neu buoc phai dung port khac.
  5. Cai plugin fail:
     - Da chay admin? OBS 64-bit? Du VC++ 2015-2022 x64?
     - Duong dan OBS co bin\64bit\obs64.exe?
